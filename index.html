Method for each batch

  1. Peel, wash and chop the ginger. Saute in sesame oil on medium-low heat until the raw smell goes and it turns lightly golden. Work in 2 to 3 lots.
  2. Dry-roast coriander, cumin and fenugreek. Roast the red chillies in a little oil until aromatic.
  3. Soak tamarind in warm water, extract the pulp and heat with jaggery until dissolved. Strain if needed.
  4. Grind ginger, chillies, spices, tamarind-jaggery and salt to a thick, slightly coarse paste. Add water a little at a time.
  5. Heat the remaining oil, splutter the mustard seeds and pour over the chutney. Simmer gently for a few minutes.
  6. Add about 80% of the salt and chilli first. Taste and adjust at the end.
  7. Cool quickly, pack in clean airtight labelled containers and refrigerate. Not shelf-stable.
